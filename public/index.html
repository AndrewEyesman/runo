    THE HOUSE RULES

    Keep your friends.
    Lose your cards.

    Match the top card’s color or symbol. First to an empty hand wins.

    • Call UNO with one card left, on your turn, before you play it. Forget, and you draw two cards and lose your turn. Your last card stays in your hand.
    • Draw one if you can’t play (or don’t want to). Drawing always ends your turn, even if the new card is playable.
    • ⊘ Skip skips the next player. ↺ Reverse changes direction; with two players, it acts as a skip.
    • +2 / +4 makes the next player draw and miss their turn. No stacking. Wild and +4 can be played on any color during your turn.
    • Wild lets you choose the next color. A final action card still takes effect.
    • Disconnected players keep their seats and turns. The host can remove a player; leaving or removal during a round ends that round.
